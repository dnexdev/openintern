Privacy

OpenIntern has no accounts, no login and no cookies. This page lists everything that is stored and who handles it.

What we store

  • Applied marks live only in your browser’s localStorage. Clearing site data removes them. We never see them.
  • Job corpus: public internship listings (titles, locations, apply links, a short excerpt of at most 400 characters) read from employer job boards and careers sites. For a few employers whose sites refuse automated requests, the maintainer saves the public listing page by hand. The full source list is in the README.
  • Request logs: our host, Vercel, records standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, path) to serve the site, rate-limit the API and stop abuse. On our plan Vercel keeps runtime logs for about an hour. We do not keep our own copy.
  • Page-view counts (only when enabled): Vercel Web Analytics, loaded from this site, without cookies, counting page views, referrers, countries and device types. It does not build personal profiles.

Who processes data

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the site and API.
  • GitHub hosts the code, the issue tracker and the daily data dumps.
  • Company logos are not loaded from third parties; fonts are served from this site.

Lawful basis

Request metadata is processed for our legitimate interest in running a free service securely. We don’t sell or share personal data.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, identity cookies, or email collection on the board.
  • Apply links go to the employer; we don’t proxy applications.

Contact

Questions, data requests and takedowns: see DATA.md or open an issue on github.com/dnexdev/openintern.
